## The single real adapter, and its exact failure

The highest-confidence production adapter that exists is the **0x Swap API affiliate fee** (`swapFeeBps` → `swapFeeRecipient`, on-chain, no manual payout) [public 2026]. It is rejected — and with it every ecosystem — for a production-verifiable reason:

- It **passes** legal (permissionless), technical (public API), and implementation (one engineer, one afternoon, instant on-chain settlement) tests.
- It is **defensible against incumbents** (a standing program 0x itself runs; 0x cannot "eliminate" your fee recipient).
- It **fails the absolute rule and the Adapter Test**: it earns only on swaps routed through a surface Satelink controls. Satelink controls no external demand surface. Activating it requires *creating* one (users, an app, an adopted framework) — which is demand creation and marketing, forbidden by the first absolute rule.

If the best, most permissionless, most instantly-buildable adapter on Earth returns $0 for Satelink, no weaker adapter can do better. Everything else is rejected a fortiori.

## The exact economic law that prevents it

**The Law of Compensated Inputs (intermediary no-free-lunch):**

> Revenue is compensation for supplying a scarce input to a flow. The only scarce inputs an intermediary can supply are: (1) routed demand, (2) supplied capacity, (3) deployed capital, or (4) an embedded/chosen service seat. An entity that supplies none of these supplies nothing scarce and is therefore paid nothing — by conservation of value.

Corollary, production-evidenced: standing programmatic revenue-share programs (0x/1inch/Jupiter affiliate fees; PFOF rebates) are permissionless to join and settle automatically — but each pays for input (1), **routed demand**: "the retail broker-dealer must originate and route the customer orders to earn" [public]; the 0x fee is charged "on swaps through your app" [public 2026]. No owned demand → the permissionless mechanism yields exactly $0.

Application: Satelink's absolute rules forbid supplying **all four** inputs — no demand creation/marketing (¬1), self-supply converts at ~$0 (¬2), no capital (¬3), no recruitment/sales/convincing (¬4). An entity that has contractually forbidden itself from providing any compensated input cannot be compensated. The impossibility is not in the market; it is in the constraint set. **The strategy "earn from existing paid economies while supplying nothing" is a perpetual-motion machine for revenue and violates conservation of value.**

## Why this is not defeatism

The adapters are real and profitable — for anyone who supplies one input. 0x affiliate fees fund real wallet apps; PFOF funds real brokers; marketplace supply funds real providers. The finding is precise and narrow: **the specific principal "Satelink, forbidden from supplying demand, supply, capital, or a chosen seat" is the empty set of earners.** The blocker is self-imposed, and therefore the only thing that changes the verdict is **relaxing exactly one absolute rule to supply exactly one input** — a founder decision, outside this M&A team's mandate, and explicitly the thing the mission forbade us from recommending.

Reject every ecosystem, including the strongest (0x/DEX affiliate-fee). No adapter satisfies all absolute rules.
