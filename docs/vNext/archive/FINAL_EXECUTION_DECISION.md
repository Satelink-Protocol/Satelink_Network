# Final Execution Decision — Phase 0.3

> The single investment decision before engineering. One verdict. No hedge, no ranking, no alternatives. Reasoning and rejected options are in `INVESTMENT_COMMITTEE_VERDICT.md`.

---

# DECISION: **WAIT**

Deploy **$0** of build capital now. Do not start production engineering. Hold the near-zero-cost positioning and activate **only** when the measurable trigger below fires.

**Why WAIT is the decision (one line):** revenue cannot be produced by engineering today — it is gated on an external market event that has not occurred — but the option to capture it is nearly free to hold and has credible, institutionally-backed upside, so the capital-protecting move is to wait, not to build and not to abandon.

---

## The measurable trigger (WAIT → BUILD)

Convert to **BUILD** the instant **all** of these are objectively true:

1. **Real flow.** A permissionless machine-settlement substrate (x402 or a successor with permissionless intermediary entry) sustains **> $100,000 / day of verified, non-wash, fee-bearing volume** in fragmentable, value-dense categories (agent-consumed data and/or inference).
2. **Sustained.** That level holds for **≥ 60 consecutive days** — not a spike, not a launch bump.
3. **Verified real.** The volume is confirmed non-wash by independent on-chain analysis (self-dealing and wash-trading excluded), not by headline transaction counts.
4. **Seat still open.** At least one permissionless router/aggregator/settlement seat remains un-enclosed — i.e., no incumbent (Coinbase, Stripe, a card network, a dominant aggregator) has captured a controlling share of that flow such that permissionless entry is foreclosed.

When 1–4 hold, the Phase 0.1 canonical architecture becomes a GO: build the substrate-agnostic Seat-B core (float/top-up-fee capture, fragmented value-dense supply, self-supply as supplier #1), reusing the live inbound settlement path.

## The abandon condition (WAIT → ABANDON)

Convert to **ABANDON** if, by the **review horizon of 2028-07 (24 months)**, **either**:

- the trigger has **not** fired (real permissionless demand never crossed the threshold), **or**
- the permissionless intermediary seat has been **enclosed** (real agent commerce consolidated onto gatekept fiat rails — OpenAI/Stripe/Visa/Mastercard — or a dominant permissionless aggregator took the seat), such that condition 4 can no longer be met.

At that point the option's value has gone to ~0 and continued holding has an opportunity cost. Abandon cleanly, redeploy attention.

## Standing posture while waiting (this is the entire permitted activity)

- **Capital deployed: $0.** No team, no build sprints, no marketing, no outreach.
- **Hold, do not extend:** the existing live inbound settlement and the small amount of already-built infrastructure are left as-is [repo]. Nothing new ships to production.
- **Monitor cheaply, quarterly:** re-run the Phase 0.2 substrate census against the trigger — measure real (non-wash) daily volume in value-dense permissionless categories and the enclosure status of the intermediary seat. This is a reading task, not a building task; a solo founder can do it in a day per quarter.
- **No constraint may be silently relaxed.** If the founder ever chooses to break "no sales" (fiat cross-protocol BD) or "no capital" (solver/liquidity), that is a *different* venture requiring a *new* investment decision and a team/capital the solo-founder gate says this thesis does not have — it is out of scope for this decision.

## Capital-protection summary

- **Downside protected:** $0 is exposed to an unproven market. Building now would risk build capital against a market that may never form (Q11: engineering cannot create the first dollar).
- **Upside retained:** the option to occupy the Seat-B position the moment real permissionless demand appears is held for ~free, with a pre-committed activation plan so no reaction time is lost.
- **Discipline enforced:** a hard 24-month horizon prevents indefinite zombie-holding; enclosure or timeout triggers a clean ABANDON.

**WAIT. Build nothing until the trigger fires. Abandon on timeout or enclosure. Deploy $0 today.**
