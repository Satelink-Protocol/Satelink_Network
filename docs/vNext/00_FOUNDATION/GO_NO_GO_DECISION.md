# GO / NO-GO Decision — Phase 0.2

> Final output of the falsification pass. One decision, evidence-backed, no optimism.

## DECISION: **NO-GO (today), with a defined revisit trigger.**

Do **not** proceed to engineering a production business. The architecture, exactly as constrained (autonomous recurring revenue from **existing** paid economies, with **no capital, no sales, no marketing, and no waiting for demand**), targets a region of the real market that is **empty at economic equilibrium** — proven structurally by the Enclosure Theorem (`MARKET_KILL_REPORT.md`) and empirically by 2026 data on both fiat and stablecoin agentic rails.

This is not a confidence downgrade. It is a **falsification**: the mandate's core premise — that such a business can be entered today — is disproven.

## Why (compressed)

1. **Every ecosystem with real recurring revenue today has enclosed revenue seats** (OpenAI ACP 4% gatekeeper; Visa/Mastercard networks owning settlement, and Visa building the reputation + registry seats itself; OpenRouter's network effect; solver capital moats). Entry requires sales, license, or capital — all forbidden. [public 2026]
2. **The only permissionless, zero-capital rail (x402) has no real demand yet** — real volume is tiny and ~half wash-traded; analysts say demand "has not emerged." Entering it today = "hoping demand comes later," explicitly a rejection criterion. [public 2026]
3. **These two sets do not overlap**, and the theorem says they *cannot* overlap while the market is at equilibrium: un-enclosed seats are un-enclosed precisely because no real revenue flows through them yet.

## What must NOT happen next

- Do not build the production loop "to be ready." That is holding a call option while telling yourself it is a business; if pursued, label it honestly as an option (see below), not as revenue.
- Do not relax a constraint silently. Each relaxation changes which architecture is viable and must be a recorded, deliberate founder decision.
- Do not re-run the same search hoping for a different answer. The answer changes only when the *market* changes (trigger below) or a *constraint* changes.

## The real decision surface (for the founder — truth, not a pitch)

The empty quadrant means progress requires **deliberately breaking exactly one rule.** Ranked by fidelity to the mission:

If the founder will break **no** rule, the correct action is **stop** and monitor.

## Revisit trigger (the only thing that flips NO-GO → GO without breaking a rule)

Re-run Phase 0.2 automatically when **a permissionless machine-settlement substrate crosses a real-demand threshold**, defined concretely as: sustained **>$100k/day of non-wash, fee-bearing volume** in fragmentable, value-dense categories (agent-consumed data / inference), verifiable by on-chain analysis rather than headline counts. At that moment x402 (or a successor) would satisfy criterion #5 without Satelink having waited *on Satelink's dime* — the market did the waiting. Only then does the canonical Seat-B architecture become a GO.

Watch also (may cross the trigger first): Mastercard **AP4M** machine-to-machine microtransactions, and whether any agentic rail opens a permissionless intermediary seat.

## Bottom line

The architecture survived Phase 0.1 (it is the right *shape*). It does **not** survive Phase 0.2 (there is no *market* to put it in yet). The honest, non-optimistic verdict is: **NO-GO now; build nothing; hold a near-zero-cost watch; revisit the moment a permissionless substrate shows real demand.** Truth over sunk cost.
