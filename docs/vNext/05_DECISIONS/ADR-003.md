# ADR-003: Spread via Merchant-of-Record Resale — No Fees, No Tokens, No New Contracts

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-07-20)
- **Deciders:** vNext architecture reset

## Context

There are three known ways an intermediary captures value in machine-commerce:
(a) **fee-skimming** — sit in the settlement path and take a percentage (requires the ecosystem's cooperation or a protocol position we do not hold);
(b) **token mechanics** — emissions/staking (the DePIN play; this repo ran it: zero external nodes, incentives code unused [measured/code]);
(c) **merchant-of-record resale** — buy upstream at the supplier's listed price on the supplier's rail, sell downstream at our own listed price on our own rail; spread = difference.

Scorecard Q4 ("join WITHOUT changing economics") structurally rules out (a): skimming inserts Satelink into other parties' settlement, changing their economics and requiring their permission. Measured history rules out (b). Option (c) requires nobody's permission, changes nobody's prices, and is invisible to both sides — the supplier sees a normal paying customer; the payer sees a normal merchant [protocol: x402 imposes no counterparty distinction].

A related decision: whether spread capture needs new smart contracts (escrow, splitters, on-chain routing). Facts: `contracts/` is frozen by standing rule; the settlement flows needed (receive USDC on Base via facilitator; send USDC on Base; later plain USDT transfers on Polygon; deposits via the already-deployed RevenueVaultV2) are all achievable with existing deployed infrastructure [code: vault live; protocol: x402].

## Decision

1. Satelink captures value exclusively as merchant-of-record: full price in (`amount_in`), full cost out (`amount_out`), spread recorded off-chain in the append-only ledger with both on-chain tx references.
2. No protocol fees, no revenue-share contracts with suppliers, no token, in any vNext phase.
3. **Zero new smart contracts.** vNext uses: the CDP facilitator (rail #1), plain ERC-20 transfers, and the already-deployed RevenueVaultV2 (rail #2 inbound). Any future proposal for a contract must reopen this ADR with a flow that provably cannot be done off-chain.
4. Liability acknowledgment: merchant-of-record means Satelink owns downstream failures (paid us, upstream broke). Handled by settlement-order invariants + refund/no-charge semantics (`SETTLEMENT_ENGINE.md`, Rule #14) — not by disclaiming responsibility.

## Consequences

- Positive: permissionless entry into any qualifying ecosystem; no contract audit surface; no token regulatory surface; spread is fully accounted and chain-verifiable without on-chain state of our own.
- Negative: spread is not protocol-enforced — a payer and supplier who discover each other can disintermediate us. Accepted: our retention mechanism is being the best router (price/reliability/aggregation), not a toll gate. This is the honest version of a moat this team can actually build.
- Negative: merchant-of-record carries the quality risk of every routed unit. Priced into margins (PRICING_ENGINE) and bounded by caps.
